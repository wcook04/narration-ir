# Origins

Narration IR was developed by Will Cook to preserve the intended meaning of
recorded explanations through an animation handoff. This skill and its fictional
example are Apache-2.0; the copied folder contains its license and notice.

The folder follows the [Agent Skills specification](https://agentskills.io/specification).
The [Remotion skill router](https://github.com/remotion-dev/skills/tree/a2824c67f8b4585c294a8101db9f9f0ad3c643f8)
informed selective reference loading; the [Manim video skill](https://github.com/vumichien/manim-skill/tree/70ccd68cf4dea135973f899b25fb408ddf5946c5)
informed the explicit artifact handoff. Their implementations are not copied,
required, installed, or represented as tested integrations by this skill.

The example has no real recording. Its sources, times, failed treatment and repair
are authored demonstration data. There is no benchmark showing that the skill
improves viewer learning or retention. See the review guide for how to evaluate
that question without conflating extra effort with the value of the representation.
