# Fictional interview editing case

This is authored demonstration data. No person was interviewed and no recording
was made. The numbered stages and time budget illustrate an explanation plan.

## Original account

The speaker says: “I saved time [two-second pause] for this interview.”
The speaker reports one experience, not measured time savings across interviews.

## Proposed edit

The editor removes only the two-second pause. The accepted words remain:
“I saved time for this interview.” The phrase “for this interview” limits the
claim to the speaker's example. Removing that phrase would broaden the claim.

## Review sequence

First preserve the source recording. Read the transcript in context, identify
the qualification, propose the cut, compare the same words before and after,
then listen to the cut in context. Word timestamps can locate a passage but
cannot establish that the edited meaning or sound is acceptable.

## Intended conclusion

A shorter pause can make this example shorter without changing its words.
The editor must still review meaning and sound. This example does not establish
that all pauses are unnecessary or that AI can autonomously certify an edit.
